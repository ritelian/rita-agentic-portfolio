04 / ABOUT & RESEARCH

A designer’s eye.

I’m Rita, a designer and educator based in the Algarve, Portugal, working at the intersection of learning, AI and digital experience.

My experience brings together AI learning experience design for international programmes, adult training at IEFP, visual arts and digital literacy teaching, and digital communications and web design.

My master’s research in Visual Arts Education at Universidade Lusófona explored generative AI as a pedagogical tool. I am pursuing a PhD in Digital Media-Art at the University of Algarve, focused on human–AI interaction and digital creative systems.
