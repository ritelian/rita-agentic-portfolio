# Review before you share

Newly written fictional demonstration for Rita Martins' portfolio. Not a client course or deliverable.

## Audience and context
Team leads beginning to use generative AI for workplace communication. Prerequisite: familiarity with reading meeting notes; no technical AI knowledge required.

## Performance objective
Given a fictional source note and AI-style summary, identify all unsupported claims, rewrite the summary without adding information, and justify the changes.

## 45-minute journey
- Frame — 5 min: compare source and summary; surface assumptions.
- Model — 10 min: facilitator demonstrates claim-by-claim verification.
- Practise — 20 min: learners annotate a second example and write a revised summary.
- Assess — 10 min: learners submit their revision and justification.

## Worked example
Source: The team discussed a pilot. A decision will follow a review next week. No budget or launch date was approved.
Flawed summary: The team approved the pilot and its budget. The launch will take place next week.

Expected revision: The team discussed a pilot and will review it next week before making a decision. No budget or launch date has been approved.

## Independent practice
Source: Five colleagues tried a draft guide. Two reported difficulty finding the help page. The team agreed to review navigation; no revised design has been approved.
Flawed summary: All colleagues found the guide easy to use. The redesigned navigation was approved for launch.

Expected findings: No support for universal ease of use, a completed redesign, approval or launch. The revision should retain the small sample and reported navigation issue.

## Assessment rubric
- Accuracy: identifies every unsupported claim.
- Revision: preserves the source meaning without adding facts.
- Reasoning: explicitly connects corrections to the source.
Feedback should identify a claim, show the evidence and explain the correction. Invite revision where a criterion is not met.

## Facilitation and access
Provide source and summary as selectable text. Do not rely on colour annotation alone. Accept typed or spoken justifications where appropriate. Offer a worked example and extra practice if prerequisites are missing. Discuss how to request review rather than guessing when source evidence is incomplete.

## Proposed evaluation
Check immediate performance using the rubric. Later use a new example to assess transfer. Record common errors to improve the lesson. No learning outcomes have been measured for this demonstration.
