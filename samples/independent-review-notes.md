# Independent demonstration: review notes

Created for Rita Martins' personal portfolio using fictional source material. This document is not an employer's assessment rubric, audit record or project deliverable. No confidential source files were used for this sample.

## Design intent
Help a learner distinguish supported claims from unsupported additions in a short summary. This is a prototype; no learner testing or impact measurement has been conducted.

## Fictional source
The team discussed a pilot. A decision will follow a review next week. No budget or launch date was approved.

## Deliberately flawed draft
The team approved the pilot and its budget. The launch will take place next week.

## Review findings
1. Pilot approval: unsupported. A decision is still pending.
2. Budget approval: contradicted by the source.
3. Launch next week: unsupported. Next week refers to the review.

## Possible revision
The team discussed a pilot and will review it next week before making a decision. No budget or launch date has been approved.

## Learner feedback
Locate each claim in the source. If the source does not support it, remove it or explain that verification is needed. Keep uncertainty visible. Then explain your corrections using the source evidence.

## Design checks for this personal sample
- Does the practice require the behaviour stated in the objective?
- Can every statement in the expected revision be traced to the source?
- Are source and draft available as selectable text, without colour-only cues?
- Is feedback available after the learner makes an attempt?
- Does a separate example test transfer rather than memorisation?

These checks are newly written for this example. They are not a claim of independent certification or evidence of a production workflow.
