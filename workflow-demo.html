    Practice task

    Compare a fictional meeting note with an AI-style summary. Identify unsupported claims, rewrite the summary and explain your decision.

    Source note · Fictional

    The team discussed a pilot. A decision will follow a review next week. No budget or launch date was approved.

    Draft summary · Deliberately flawed

    The team approved the pilot and its budget. The launch will take place next week.

    Assessment criteria

    • Identifies the unsupported approval, budget and launch claims.
    • Rewrites the summary without adding information.
    • Explains why verification is needed before sharing.
